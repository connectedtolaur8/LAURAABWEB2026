About

French graphic designer and art director specialising in print and digital media. I have a keen interest in editorial design and typography, but I also explore the possibilities offered by digital tools and their influence on contemporary graphic forms.

I have spent the last few years exploring graphic communication and art direction, first as a freelancer [2019–2022], then at a studio in Paris [2022–2025]. Since 2025, I have been working as a freelancer again in Paris.
